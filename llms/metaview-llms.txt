# Metaview

> Metaview is the Agentic Recruiting Platform. AI agents for getting the right people in seat — source, engage, and hire better with agents that truly get the brief.

## Core Platform

- [Metaview: Agentic Recruiting Platform](https://www.metaview.ai): AI agents for recruiting. Source, engage, and hire better with agents that understand your hiring needs. Trusted by 5,000+ organizations.
- [AI Sourcing Agent](https://www.metaview.ai/sourcing): Truly autonomous AI sourcing agent. Finds candidates 24/7 by interpreting your hiring needs from intake calls, JDs, resumes, or natural language. Learns from your feedback and calibrates over time.
- [Application Review](https://www.metaview.ai/application-review): AI-native inbound candidate screening. Reviews 100% of applicants against your Ideal Candidate Profile, surfaces the strongest matches with reasoning, and self-calibrates as you make decisions. Includes fraud detection.
- [AI Interview Notes](https://www.metaview.ai/interview-notes): AI notetaker built specifically for recruiting. Joins calls, captures every signal, and delivers structured notes the moment the call ends. Fully configurable to your hiring workflow.
- [Outreach Sequences](https://www.metaview.ai/outreach): Outreach sequences built into your sourcing flow. AI-personalized multi-step email sequences with verified contact data. Get 2x candidate replies.
- [Recruiting Reports](https://www.metaview.ai/reports): Fully customizable AI reporting engine. Surface trends, answer questions, and get alerts across your entire hiring process in real time.
- [AI Job Posts](https://www.metaview.ai/job-posts): Generate compliant, consistent, and on-brand job descriptions. AI-powered checks and suggestions to improve job post quality.
- [Intake & Debrief Notes](https://www.metaview.ai/intake-debrief-notes): Capture key details from intake meetings and debriefs automatically.
